Sub-initiative

Dialogue Without Division

Different opinions. One safe space. We do not have to agree to learn from one another.

Young people need spaces where they can discuss difficult and controversial issues without fear of being attacked, dismissed, or excluded. Our approach encourages:

Listen

Listen to understand, not simply to respond.

Reflect

Think about your own assumptions and values.

Question

Ask questions before reaching conclusions.

Discuss

Explore disagreement constructively.

Understand

Recognise that complex issues rarely have simple answers.

Respect

Respect people even when opinions differ.

Think critically

Challenge misinformation, manipulation, and polarising narratives.

Connect

Find common ground without erasing differences.

Act

Turn dialogue into meaningful participation.

Erasmus+ & European Youth Opportunities

Connecting young people across borders. Building skills, confidence and active citizenship.

At Global Youth Development Network (GYD Network), we believe that young people grow when they are given opportunities to experience new cultures, exchange ideas, develop practical skills and connect with peers beyond their own communities.

Through our Erasmus+ and European Youth Programme, we aim to build international partnerships that create meaningful learning, mobility and participation opportunities for young people and youth professionals.

Our work is aligned with the core priorities of Erasmus+: inclusion and diversity, digital transformation, environmental responsibility, and participation in democratic life and civic engagement.

What we do

Through collaboration with youth organisations, educational institutions, community groups and European partners, GYD Network seeks to develop and participate in:

Youth Exchanges

Bringing young people from different countries together to learn, exchange cultures and explore shared challenges.

Youth Worker Mobility

Enabling youth professionals and volunteers to exchange knowledge, training and good practice.

Youth Participation Activities

Giving young people a stronger voice in their communities and creating opportunities to engage with decision-makers.

DiscoverEU Inclusion Opportunities

Supporting young people with fewer opportunities to experience Europe, develop independence and broaden their horizons.

European Cooperation Partnerships

Building long-term partnerships between organisations to exchange expertise, develop innovative approaches and create sustainable youth programmes.

Training, Workshops & Peer Learning

Developing leadership, employability, digital, communication and intercultural skills.

These areas reflect the Erasmus+ youth actions described in the programme, including youth exchanges, youth-worker mobility, youth participation activities and DiscoverEU Inclusion.
Our focus
Inclusion. Participation. Skills. Connection. Opportunity.

We are particularly interested in ensuring that international opportunities reach young people who may otherwise face barriers to participation.

Erasmus+ places strong emphasis on inclusion, including young people affected by economic, social, cultural, geographical, educational or other barriers. The programme can provide additional support designed to reduce those barriers.

For GYD Network, international mobility is therefore not simply about travelling. It is about giving young people the opportunity to:

discover→connect→learn→participate→grow→lead
Why Erasmus+?

International youth experiences can help young people strengthen their confidence, employability, initiative, digital and language skills, intercultural understanding and participation in society.

For GYD Network, these opportunities directly support our mission:

To connect young people with the people, institutions, knowledge and opportunities that can help them shape their future.
