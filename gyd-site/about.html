About us

A UK-based youth development organisation with a global outlook.

Global Youth Development Organisation CIC believes every young person should have the opportunity to learn, participate, connect and lead.

Who we are

Our approach

Our work brings together youth development, education, skills, democratic participation, dialogue, culture, technology and inclusion.

We create spaces where young people can develop their potential while connecting with people and ideas beyond their immediate communities.

Mission

Give every young person a voice and a path forward.

We connect young people with the mentors, institutions, and networks that turn potential into opportunity — regardless of where they were born. We do this by building dialogue between youth and the people who make decisions that affect them.

Vision

A world where youth voice shapes real decisions.

We create spaces where young people can listen, question, learn, discuss, disagree respectfully, participate and lead.

We work toward a future where young people are not just beneficiaries of programmes designed for them, but active participants in the institutions, policies, and partnerships that shape their communities — locally and globally.

Our values

What guides the work.

Voice

Young people deserve to be heard.

Inclusion

Everyone should have the opportunity to participate.

Respect

We value people, perspectives and differences.

Curiosity

We encourage questions, learning and discovery.

Critical Thinking

We question, reflect and seek understanding.

Participation

Young people should have meaningful opportunities to take part.

Empowerment

We help young people develop confidence, skills and agency.

Global Citizenship

We connect local action with global understanding.
